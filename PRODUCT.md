# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: a Metro Detroit homeowner in the middle of a loss — water in the basement, a burst pipe, sewage backup, mold found, or fire/smoke damage. Usually on a phone, often stressed, sometimes at night. The job: confirm this company covers their area, trust it quickly, and call. Secondary audiences (landlords, property managers, commercial owners) exist and have their own pages (/commercial/, /commercial-mitigation-plan/), but the design serves the emergency homeowner first.

## Product Purpose

Marketing and lead-generation site for Restoration Group of Metro Detroit (RGMD), a local restoration contractor: water damage, emergency water removal, basement flooding, sump pump failure, sewage, pipe bursts, mold remediation, fire and smoke, and reconstruction. Success is a phone call to (313) 771-3673, and second, a submitted service request form.

## Positioning

Independently owned and operated locally, one contractor from mitigation through rebuild, and documentation built for insurance claims. Owner has IICRC certifications (WRT, ASD, CRMI, CCMI) and an architecture background.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive mostly from Google search for "[service] [city]" — 9 service pages and 30 city pages are the main landing pages. Emergency response is available 24/7. Service area: select communities in Wayne, Oakland, Macomb, Livingston and Washtenaw Counties, with excluded ZIP codes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 15 App Router, static generation, deployed on Vercel.
- Every URL, page title, H1 and body copy must stay as-is (SEO migration from WordPress). Copy changes require approval.
- Lead form sends through EmailJS (components/RequestForm.tsx).
- Phone CTA must be prominent everywhere, especially on mobile.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Restoration Group of Metro Detroit (RGMD).
- Logo: public/logo.png and public/images/logo-wordmark-light.webp. Logo colors are navy (#14264a) and orange (#e2661c).
- Voice: direct, calm, practical, no hype.

## Evidence on Hand

- Stats confirmed by owner: 4.8★ Google rating, 15+ years experience, 1,000+ projects.
- Customer reviews in data/site.ts (REVIEWS), from the existing site.
- Real job photos exist but will be supplied later; design with clearly marked photo slots. Do not use stock photos presented as RGMD jobs, and do not invent testimonials, certifications, or claims.

## Product Principles

1. The phone number is the product: reachable in one tap from every screen.
2. Trust before persuasion — local, certified, documented, real.
3. Calm under pressure: clarity over decoration for someone mid-emergency.
4. Preserve every word and URL that ranks.
